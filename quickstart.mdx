## Create Your Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up">
    Go to [certwatch.app/signup](https://certwatch.app/signup) and create your account using email or Google.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create Organization">
    After signing up, you'll be prompted to create your first organization. This is your workspace for managing certificates and team members.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Onboarding">
    Follow the quick onboarding flow to set up your preferences and notification channels.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add Your First Certificate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Certificates">
    From your dashboard, click on **Certificates** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Certificate">
    Click the **Add Certificate** button in the top right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Domain">
    Enter the domain you want to monitor (e.g., `example.com`). CertWatch will automatically fetch the SSL certificate information.

    <Note>
      You can add any publicly accessible domain. CertWatch will check the certificate on port 443 by default.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Alerts">
    Choose when you want to be notified:
    - 30 days before expiry
    - 14 days before expiry
    - 7 days before expiry
    - 3 days before expiry
    - 1 day before expiry
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Add Certificate** to start monitoring.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Set Up Notifications

CertWatch supports multiple notification channels. Set up at least one to receive alerts.

### Email Notifications

Email notifications are enabled by default using your account email. You can add additional email addresses or disable them in Settings.

### Slack Integration

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Channels">
    Navigate to **Channels** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Slack Channel">
    Click **Add Channel** and select **Slack**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize">
    Click **Connect to Slack** and authorize CertWatch to post to your workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Channel">
    Choose which Slack channel should receive certificate alerts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Discord Integration

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Channels">
    Navigate to **Channels** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Discord Channel">
    Click **Add Channel** and select **Discord**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Webhook URL">
    Paste your Discord webhook URL.

    <Tip>
      To create a webhook in Discord: Server Settings → Integrations → Webhooks → New Webhook
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Verify Everything Works

Once set up, you can verify your configuration:

1. **Check Certificate Status** - Your certificate should show as "Valid" with days until expiry
2. **Test Notifications** - Use the "Send Test" button on any notification channel
3. **Review Dashboard** - Your dashboard shows an overview of all monitored certificates
