title: "Email Notifications"
description: "Configure email alerts for certificate events"
---

Email is the default notification channel in CertWatch. Every account can receive email notifications.

## Default Email

Your account email is automatically set up to receive notifications when you sign up.

## Adding Email Channels

To add additional email recipients:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Channels">
    Navigate to **Channels** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Channel">
    Click **Add Channel** and select **Email**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Email Address">
    Enter the email address that should receive notifications.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Settings">
    Choose which types of alerts this email should receive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Add Channel** to save.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Email Content

CertWatch emails include:

- Clear subject line with certificate status
- Domain name and expiration date
- Days remaining until expiry
- Direct link to view certificate in dashboard
- Unsubscribe link

### Example Email

```
Subject: [CertWatch] Certificate expiring in 7 days: example.com

The SSL certificate for example.com is expiring soon.

Domain: example.com
Status: Expiring Soon
Days Remaining: 7
Expires: January 15, 2025 at 3:00 PM UTC

View Certificate: https://certwatch.app/certificates/abc123

---
You're receiving this because you're monitoring this certificate.
Unsubscribe: https://certwatch.app/unsubscribe/...
```

## Managing Emails

### Edit Email Channel

1. Go to **Channels**
2. Find the email channel
3. Click **Edit**
4. Update settings
5. Save changes

### Disable Notifications

To temporarily stop receiving emails:

1. Go to **Channels**
2. Find the email channel
3. Toggle the **Enabled** switch off

### Delete Email Channel

1. Go to **Channels**
2. Find the email channel
3. Click **Delete**
4. Confirm deletion

<Warning>
  Deleting a channel cannot be undone. You'll need to add it again if you want to restore it.
</Warning>

## Troubleshooting

### Not Receiving Emails

1. **Check spam folder** - CertWatch emails may be filtered
2. **Verify email address** - Ensure no typos in the address
3. **Check channel status** - Make sure the channel is enabled
4. **Whitelist sender** - Add `notifications@certwatch.app` to your contacts

### Email Delays

Emails are typically delivered within seconds, but may be delayed by:

- Email provider processing
- Spam filtering systems
- High email volume periods
