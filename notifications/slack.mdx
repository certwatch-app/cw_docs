title: "Slack Integration"
description: "Get certificate alerts in Slack"
---

Connect CertWatch to Slack to receive certificate notifications directly in your workspace channels.

## Setting Up Slack

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Channels">
    Navigate to **Channels** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Slack Channel">
    Click **Add Channel** and select **Slack**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect to Slack">
    Click **Connect to Slack**. You'll be redirected to Slack to authorize CertWatch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize">
    Review the permissions and click **Allow** to authorize the integration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Channel">
    Choose which Slack channel should receive certificate alerts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Add Channel** to complete setup.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Permissions Required

CertWatch requests minimal permissions:

| Permission | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| `chat:write` | Post messages to selected channel |
| `channels:read` | List available channels for selection |

<Note>
  CertWatch cannot read your messages or access any channel content. We can only post notifications.
</Note>

## Slack Message Format

CertWatch messages are formatted for easy reading:

- Color-coded status (green, yellow, red)
- Certificate domain as title
- Expiration details in the body
- Action button to view in CertWatch

### Example Message

```
🔔 Certificate Expiring Soon

example.com is expiring in 7 days

Expires: January 15, 2025
Status: Expiring Soon

[View Certificate]
```

## Managing Slack Channels

### Change Target Channel

1. Go to **Channels**
2. Find your Slack integration
3. Click **Edit**
4. Select a different Slack channel
5. Save changes

### Test Integration

1. Go to **Channels**
2. Find your Slack integration
3. Click **Send Test**
4. Check your Slack channel for the test message

### Disconnect Slack

1. Go to **Channels**
2. Find your Slack integration
3. Click **Delete**
4. Confirm removal

## Multiple Slack Channels

You can add multiple Slack channels:

- Different channels for different certificate groups
- Separate channels for urgent vs. early warnings
- Team-specific notification routing

## Troubleshooting

### Connection Failed

- Ensure you're an admin or have permission to install apps
- Check that your Slack workspace allows third-party integrations
- Try disconnecting and reconnecting

### Messages Not Appearing

- Verify the channel is public or CertWatch was added to private channel
- Check that the integration is enabled in CertWatch
- Test the connection using the "Send Test" button

### Wrong Channel

- Edit the Slack channel in CertWatch settings
- You can change the target channel without reconnecting
