CertWatch is built for teams. Create organizations to manage certificates collaboratively and control access.

## Organizations

An organization is your team's workspace in CertWatch. Each organization has:

- Its own set of certificates
- Notification channels
- Team members with roles
- Billing and subscription

## Organization Types

### Personal

Created automatically when you sign up. Good for:

- Individual projects
- Testing and evaluation
- Personal domains

### Team

Shared workspace for multiple users:

- Invite team members
- Role-based permissions
- Shared notification channels

## Creating an Organization

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Organization Switcher">
    In the sidebar, click your current organization name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create New">
    Click **Create Organization**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Details">
    - Organization name
    - (Optional) Description
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create">
    Click **Create** to set up the new organization.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Switching Organizations

1. Click the organization name in the sidebar
2. Select the organization you want to switch to
3. The page will reload with that organization's data

## Organization Settings

Access organization settings by:

1. Going to **Settings** in the sidebar
2. Clicking **Organization** tab

### General Settings

- Organization name
- Description
- Default notification preferences

### Danger Zone

- Transfer ownership
- Delete organization

<Warning>
  Deleting an organization removes all certificates, channels, and team members. This cannot be undone.
</Warning>

## Best Practices

1. **One org per project/client** - Keep certificates organized
2. **Invite all stakeholders** - Ensure the right people get notifications
3. **Use descriptive names** - Make it easy to identify organizations
4. **Review access regularly** - Remove former team members promptly
