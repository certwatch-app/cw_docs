## Upgrading

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings">
    Navigate to **Settings** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Billing">
    Click the **Billing** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Plan">
    Select the plan you want to upgrade to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Payment">
    Enter your payment information via Stripe.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Review and confirm your subscription.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your new plan takes effect immediately.

## Downgrading

To downgrade:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Billing**
2. Click **Change Plan**
3. Select a lower tier plan
4. Confirm the change

<Warning>
  If you exceed the new plan's limits, you'll need to remove certificates or members before downgrading.
</Warning>

## Billing Cycle

- Subscriptions are billed **monthly** or **annually**
- Annual plans receive a **20% discount**
- Billing date is the day you subscribed

## Payment Methods

CertWatch accepts:

- Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express)
- Debit cards
- Some regional payment methods

All payments are processed securely via Stripe.

## Invoices

Access your invoices:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Billing**
2. Click **Billing History**
3. Download invoices as PDF

## Cancellation

To cancel your subscription:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Billing**
2. Click **Cancel Subscription**
3. Confirm cancellation

After cancellation:

- Your plan remains active until the end of the billing period
- You'll be downgraded to Free at period end
- Your data is retained (certificates over the free limit are paused)

## Refunds

- We offer refunds within 7 days of initial purchase
- Contact support@certwatch.app for refund requests
- Refunds are processed to the original payment method
