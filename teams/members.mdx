Invite your team to CertWatch to collaborate on certificate management.

## Roles

CertWatch has three roles:

| Role | Permissions |
|------|-------------|
| **Owner** | Full access, can delete organization, manage billing |
| **Admin** | Manage certificates, channels, and members |
| **Member** | View certificates and channels, receive notifications |

### Role Comparison

| Action | Owner | Admin | Member |
|--------|-------|-------|--------|
| View certificates | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Add certificates | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Delete certificates | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Manage channels | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Invite members | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Remove members | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |
| Manage billing | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Delete organization | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |

## Inviting Members

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings">
    Navigate to **Settings** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Members">
    Click the **Members** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite">
    Click **Invite Member**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Details">
    - Email address of the person to invite
    - Select their role (Admin or Member)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send Invite">
    Click **Send Invite**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The invited person will receive an email with a link to join your organization.

## Pending Invitations

View and manage pending invitations:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Members**
2. See the **Pending Invitations** section
3. Options:
   - **Resend** - Send the invitation email again
   - **Revoke** - Cancel the invitation

<Note>
  Invitations expire after 7 days. Revoke and resend if needed.
</Note>

## Managing Members

### Change Role

1. Go to **Settings** → **Members**
2. Find the member
3. Click the role dropdown
4. Select new role

<Warning>
  You cannot change the Owner's role. Transfer ownership first if needed.
</Warning>

### Remove Member

1. Go to **Settings** → **Members**
2. Find the member
3. Click **Remove**
4. Confirm removal

## Transferring Ownership

To transfer organization ownership:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Organization**
2. Scroll to **Danger Zone**
3. Click **Transfer Ownership**
4. Select the new owner (must be an existing Admin)
5. Confirm the transfer

<Warning>
  After transferring, your role becomes Admin. Only do this if you're sure.
</Warning>

## Leaving an Organization

If you're not the owner:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Members**
2. Find yourself in the list
3. Click **Leave Organization**
4. Confirm

<Note>
  Owners cannot leave. Transfer ownership first, then leave.
</Note>

## Member Limits

Member limits depend on your plan:

| Plan | Member Limit |
|------|--------------|
| Free | 1 (owner only) |
| Pro | 5 members |
| Team | 20 members |
| Enterprise | Unlimited |
