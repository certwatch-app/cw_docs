## Key Features

### Certificate Monitoring

Add any domain and CertWatch will automatically detect and monitor its SSL certificate. Track:

- **Expiration dates** - Know exactly when certificates expire
- **Certificate chain** - Verify complete chain of trust
- **Issuer information** - Track certificate authorities
- **Domain validation** - Ensure certificates match your domains

### Smart Notifications

Configure when and how you want to be notified:

- **Customizable thresholds** - Alert at 30, 14, 7, 3, and 1 days before expiry
- **Multiple channels** - Email, Slack, Discord, and webhooks
- **Team routing** - Send alerts to the right people

### Team Management

Built for teams of all sizes:

- **Organizations** - Create separate workspaces for different projects
- **Role-based access** - Admin, Member, and Viewer roles
- **Invite system** - Easy email invitations with secure tokens
