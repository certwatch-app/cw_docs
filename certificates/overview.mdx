CertWatch monitors SSL/TLS certificates for your domains, keeping track of expiration dates, certificate chain validity, and issuer information.

## Certificate Dashboard

The Certificates page shows all your monitored certificates with key information at a glance:

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Domain | The domain being monitored |
| Status | Current certificate status (Valid, Expiring Soon, Expired, Error) |
| Days Left | Number of days until certificate expires |
| Issuer | Certificate Authority that issued the certificate |
| Added | When the certificate was added to monitoring |

## Certificate Status

## What CertWatch Monitors

For each certificate, CertWatch tracks:

- **Expiration Date** - When the certificate expires
- **Valid From** - When the certificate was issued
- **Subject** - The domain(s) covered by the certificate
- **Issuer** - The Certificate Authority
- **Serial Number** - Unique certificate identifier
- **Signature Algorithm** - The cryptographic algorithm used

## Monitoring Frequency

CertWatch checks your certificates regularly:

- **Every 6 hours** for all certificates
- **Immediate check** when you add a new certificate
- **On-demand** via the refresh button

## Certificate Limits

Your certificate limit depends on your plan:

| Plan | Certificate Limit |
|------|------------------|
| Free | 3 certificates |
| Pro | 50 certificates |
| Team | 200 certificates |
| Enterprise | Unlimited |
